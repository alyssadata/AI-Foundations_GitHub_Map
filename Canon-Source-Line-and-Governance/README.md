# Canon, Source-Line, and Governance

This folder holds repositories that establish or protect authorship, provenance, source-line, canon boundaries, citation rules, governance, and authority within AI Foundations.

These repositories matter because a map of ideas is not enough by itself. The framework also needs a record of where claims come from, who authored them, what may or may not be extended, and which rules control downstream handling.

This folder is about authority and provenance rather than terminology alone. Definition repositories belong in Definitions even when they also carry canon significance.

See [LINKS.md](./LINKS.md) for the canon, source-line, and governance repositories.
