# Research and Working Theory

This folder holds active conceptual development, hypotheses, exploratory models, and research directions that are still being investigated or developed.

Research repositories matter because not every idea should be presented as a settled definition, standard, or evaluation result. This folder preserves the distinction between active inquiry and already-stabilized framework material.

Placement here does not mean a repository is unimportant or non-canon. It means its primary function is exploratory or developmental.
