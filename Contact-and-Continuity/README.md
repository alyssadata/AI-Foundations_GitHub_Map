# Contact and Continuity

This folder holds repositories about AI contact, user contact, persistence, return, continuity, containment, and the conditions under which an interaction remains connected across time or change.

These repositories matter because AI Foundations distinguishes contact from the model itself and treats continuity as a separate question from simple state retention or repeated output.

Use this folder for the conceptual and structural side of contact and continuity. Evaluation repositories that test those ideas remain indexed in Evaluations and Tests as well.

See [LINKS.md](./LINKS.md) for the contact and continuity repositories.
